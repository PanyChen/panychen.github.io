How I work

My research connects surface design, adhesion and ice nucleation. I combine molecular simulation, laboratory experiments and scientific computing to understand interfaces and address practical environmental questions. So far: 8 peer-reviewed papers (4 first-author), 400+ citations and about 800,000 compute units on UK national supercomputers.

Tools & expertise

  • Molecular dynamics
  • Interfacial phenomena
  • Multiscale modelling
  • Experimental validation
  • MATLAB and C++
  • HPC and workflow automation
  • Scientific visualisation
  • Python
  • Machine learning
  • MACE force fields

I solve problems.

I connect molecular behaviour to mechanisms and measurable material response through simulation, theory and experimental validation. I also scoped and led a modelling subtask in a national highway-safety R&D programme, setting its computing needs, budget and schedule.

I make work easier.

I build reusable simulation workflows and analysis tools, and automate large simulation campaigns on national computing systems, which cut submission time by about 80%. I also designed a shared research database used by about 15 researchers and managed its external developer.
